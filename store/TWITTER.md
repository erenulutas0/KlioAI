# X (Twitter) — ne zaman, ne yazarak

İki ayrı şey var ve karıştırılmaması gerekiyor: **ürün gönderisi** (uygulama
hesabından, videoyla) ve **yapım hikayesi** (senin hesabından, sayılarla). İkincisi
X'te birincisinden çok daha uzak gidiyor, çünkü reklam gibi durmuyor. Metinler
İngilizce, bu dosya Türkçe.

Her ikisinde de geçerli üç kural:

- **Linki gönderinin içine koyma.** X dış linkli gönderileri daha az dağıtıyor.
  Link, kendi gönderine attığın **ilk yanıta** girer.
- **Videoyu doğrudan yükle.** TikTok linki paylaşma, izlenme oradan gelmez.
- Videoya **alt metin** ekle (görsele tıkla → ALT). Hem erişilebilirlik hem
  aramada karşılığı var.

## 2. Yapım hikayesi — senin hesabından, altı gönderilik dizi

Bu dizinin tamamı doğru sayılardan kuruldu ve hepsi bu projeden. Paylaşmak
istemediğin bir rakam varsa çıkar — ama **yuvarlama**, dizinin tüm değeri
rakamların gerçek olmasından geliyor.

```
1/ I built an English speaking app on my own. Here are the real numbers, because the honest ones are more useful than the ones people usually post.

105 accounts. 11 of them ever opened it on a second day.
```

```
2/ The store page was not the problem. Play showed it 311 times in 28 days, and 19% of the people who reached the page installed it. That conversion is fine.

Eleven impressions a day is not.
```

```
3/ The sign-in screen was a problem. The app opened on "continue with Google" before it had said a single word to anybody.

It now opens a guest account on first launch and goes straight into a conversation. You sign in when you want to keep what you did.
```

```
4/ Notifications too. The system prompt opened at launch, before anything had happened, and Android closes that setting for good after two refusals.

12 push tokens across 105 accounts. The reminders meant to bring people back could not reach nine in ten of them.
```

```
5/ It asks after your third turn in a conversation now, and only if you say yes to being reminded first.

None of this can be measured yet, because almost nobody arrives. Distribution is the whole problem, and it's the part I'm worst at.
```

```
6/ What the app does: you talk out loud to a character -- a waiter, a receptionist. When a word isn't there, you say that part in your own language and the conversation keeps going. Then it shows you the English you needed, and why.
```

**İlk yanıt:**

```
KlioAI, free on Google Play: <link>
```

6. gönderiye videoyu ekle. 1-5 düz metin kalsın; dizinin başına görsel koymak
ilk gönderinin okunma oranını düşürüyor.
